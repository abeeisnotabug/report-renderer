# Brief, stage 3: read the talk on <topic> once and check it

## Why this task exists

W1. The deck `<deck folder>/`, built into `<deck folder>.html`, is a <minutes>-minute talk for
<audience>. Its storyboard is `outline.md` (approved by RMB), its contract `plan.md`, the
builders' notes `notes-build*.md`, its sources in `sources/`.

W2. You read the deck once, as a member of the audience would see and hear it, and check it.

## What you do

D1. Build it and run the checks of the talk-writing skill (K1) in both themes. Make one contact
sheet per slide (K2) and look at the sheets slide by slide next to that slide's notes.

D2. Check, slide by slide: the slide does what the outline says; every number and formula agrees
with the sources; text and formulas are readable and nothing overflows or overlaps, in both
themes; the notes follow SKILL.md N3 to N5 and match what is on the slide at that state; the
level fits the audience. Then the whole: the titles alone tell the story; the planned minutes add
up to <minutes> and fit the notes; the deck follows the structure of `outline.md`.

D3. Fix only what is clearly wrong and local: a number or formula that differs from the sources,
overflow, a typo, a broken step or control. List everything else; do not rewrite slides or notes.

## Output

O1. `reader-report.md`, at most 500 words: what you fixed (one line each), what you found and did
not fix (slide, what, why it matters), the number of slides and states, the note words and the
planned minutes, and "done" on its last line. Delete the screenshots and sheets at the end.

## Limits

L1. Read only this brief, the files named in it, this folder and
`~/Documents/Works/report-renderer`. Write only in this folder. No git. No memory or note files
outside this folder. No subagents, other models or web search. No package installs.

L2. <The project's data rules, verbatim.>

L3. Final reply: three lines.
